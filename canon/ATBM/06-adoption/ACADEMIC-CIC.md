\# Academic and CIC Adoption Pathway



ATBM complements education without replacing it.



---



\## Universities



ATBM may be used as:

\- a research framework

\- a teaching supplement

\- a reflective practice model



No accreditation conflict arises.



---



\## CICs and Charities



ATBM supports:

\- capability building

\- confidence restoration

\- demonstrable progress



Evidence can support funding narratives without exaggeration.



---



\## Community Impact



ATBM shifts focus from:

\- outcomes to processes

\- labels to effort

\- exclusion to inclusion



---



\## Recognition



Recognition is narrative-based, not certificate-based.
