\# Risk and Compliance Posture



ATBM is designed to reduce, not increase, institutional risk.



---



\## Regulatory Neutrality



ATBM:

\- does not issue qualifications

\- does not store personal data by default

\- does not replace statutory frameworks



---



\## Data Protection



\- evidence remains locally controlled

\- cloud use is optional

\- offline teaching is supported

\- GDPR principles are respected by design



---



\## Safeguarding



ATBM prohibits:

\- coercive participation

\- performance ranking

\- punitive use of evidence



Safeguarding responsibility remains institutional.



---



\## Procurement Safety



ATBM can be adopted as:

\- open methodology

\- internal framework

\- pilot programme



No vendor lock-in.



---



\## Auditability



All ATBM processes are:

\- documented

\- inspectable

\- reproducible



This is deliberate.



---



\## Exit Strategy



Institutions may disengage at any time without data loss.
