\# ATBM Adoption Modes



Institutions may adopt ATBM in discrete modes.



No exclusivity is required.



---



\## Mode A — Observational



\- ATBM used as a reference framework

\- No operational change

\- No data ingestion



Use case:

\- universities

\- inspectorates

\- policy review teams



---



\## Mode B — Educational Supplement



\- ATBM taught alongside existing curricula

\- No certification replacement

\- Evidence remains learner-owned



Use case:

\- prisons

\- CICs

\- community education



---



\## Mode C — Evidence Companion



\- ATBM evidence artefacts run in parallel

\- No system-of-record conflict

\- Exportable at all times



Use case:

\- probation

\- employability programmes



---



\## Mode D — Programme Spine



\- ATBM provides structural backbone

\- Institution retains governance

\- ATBM remains methodology, not authority



Use case:

\- rehabilitation pilots

\- capability academies



---



\## Principle



Adoption must never create dependency.
