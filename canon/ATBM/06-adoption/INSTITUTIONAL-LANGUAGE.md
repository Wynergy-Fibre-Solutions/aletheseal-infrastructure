\# Institutional Language Guide



To avoid misunderstanding, institutions should use precise language.



---



\## Use These Terms



\- framework

\- methodology

\- learning model

\- evidence system



---



\## Avoid These Terms



\- qualification

\- certification

\- compliance tool

\- assessment replacement



---



\## Why This Matters



Language determines risk perception.



ATBM is intentionally conservative in claims.
