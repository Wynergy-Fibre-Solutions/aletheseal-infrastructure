\# Prison and Probation Adoption Pathway



ATBM is particularly suited to custodial and post-custodial settings.



---



\## Entry Point



\- voluntary participation

\- no behavioural scoring

\- focus on learning and evidence



---



\## During Custody



Learners:

\- document learning

\- reflect on capability

\- create sealed artefacts



No internet dependency is required.



---



\## Transition Phase



Upon release:

\- evidence travels with the individual

\- no institutional permission required

\- continuity is preserved



---



\## Probation Alignment



ATBM evidence may:

\- supplement supervision discussions

\- demonstrate engagement

\- support employment readiness



It must not be used punitively.



---



\## Ethical Boundary



ATBM must never be used to:

\- extend sentences

\- determine parole

\- enforce compliance



This boundary is non-negotiable.
