\# ATBM Exit, Refusal, and Non-Participation Framework



This document defines refusal, exit, and non-participation as protected rights within ATBM.



---



\## Core Position



Participation must never be required for dignity, safety, or legitimacy.



A system that punishes refusal is coercive.



---



\## Right to Refuse



Any participant may refuse:

\- participation

\- continuation

\- escalation

\- instruction

\- evaluation



Refusal requires no justification.



---



\## Non-Participation Is Neutral



Choosing not to participate:

\- carries no penalty

\- implies no fault

\- triggers no suspicion



Non-participation is a valid state.



---



\## Exit as a Safeguard



Exit exists to:

\- prevent coercion

\- preserve agency

\- maintain ethical integrity



Exit is not failure.

Exit is protection.



---



\## Safe Exit Requirements



All exit paths must be:

\- clearly documented

\- accessible at all times

\- non-punitive

\- reversible where appropriate



A hidden exit is not an exit.



---



\## Exit Without Retaliation



No exit may result in:

\- loss of unrelated rights

\- reputational harm

\- informal punishment

\- future exclusion



Retaliation invalidates consent.



---



\## Partial Exit and Pausing



Participants may:

\- pause engagement

\- disengage from specific components

\- return later without penalty



Participation is modular by design.



---



\## Authority Limits on Exit



Authority may:

\- ensure safe disengagement

\- preserve shared assets

\- document completion state



Authority may not:

\- prevent exit

\- delay exit

\- condition exit on compliance



---



\## Refusal in Power-Imbalanced Contexts



In environments such as:

\- prisons

\- probation

\- education

\- care settings



Refusal must be:

\- explicitly protected

\- actively reaffirmed

\- clearly separated from punishment



---



\## Record of Exit



Exit records:

\- document state only

\- do not assign blame

\- do not follow participants



Records exist for system learning, not control.



---



\## System Integrity Check



If exit:

\- creates fear

\- damages future options

\- triggers scrutiny



Then the system has failed and must correct.



---



\## Final Principle



The right to say no is non-negotiable.



Any system worthy of trust must survive refusal.



---



End of exit, refusal, and non-participation framework.
