\# ATBM Institutional Interface



\## Purpose



This document explains how ATBM can be engaged with by institutions

without surrendering independence, authorship, or ethical control.



ATBM does not integrate into institutions.

Institutions interface with ATBM.



---



\## What ATBM Is (Institutional Framing)



ATBM is:

\- a trust baseline model

\- a method for producing inspectable systems

\- a framework for long-term evidence continuity

\- an educational and stewardship architecture



ATBM is not:

\- a political programme

\- a compliance product

\- a certification authority

\- a proprietary control system



---



\## Suitable Institutional Contexts



ATBM is appropriate for:

\- education services

\- rehabilitation and reintegration programmes

\- probation and post-release support

\- academic research and teaching

\- civic technology and public interest systems



It is not designed for:

\- enforcement

\- surveillance

\- punitive monitoring

\- behavioural coercion



---



\## Modes of Engagement



Institutions may engage ATBM in the following ways:



\### 1. Reference Mode

Using ATBM concepts to inform:

\- policy thinking

\- system design

\- ethical review



No adoption.

No endorsement required.



---



\### 2. Teaching Mode

Using ATBM materials for:

\- curriculum support

\- skills education

\- critical thinking development



Teaching does not require certification.

Teaching must respect ATBM boundaries.



---



\### 3. Pilot Mode

Applying ATBM methods in:

\- limited programmes

\- voluntary participation contexts

\- non-coercive environments



All pilots must remain inspectable and reversible.



---



\### 4. Observation Mode

Independent bodies may:

\- observe ATBM processes

\- review evidence lineage

\- audit integrity claims



Observation does not imply governance.



---



\## Governance Boundary



ATBM governance remains:

\- independent

\- public-facing

\- evidence-based

\- stewarded, not owned



Institutions do not:

\- set ATBM doctrine

\- appoint ATBM stewards

\- control evolution of the canon



---



\## Legal and Ethical Alignment



ATBM is designed to align with:

\- human rights principles

\- data protection law

\- academic freedom norms

\- safeguarding obligations



Alignment does not equal submission.



---



\## Risk and Safeguards



ATBM includes safeguards against:

\- institutional capture

\- mission drift

\- credential inflation

\- reputational misuse



Any misuse invalidates engagement.



---



\## Exit and Reversibility



Institutions must be able to:

\- disengage cleanly

\- retain their own data

\- publish critical findings



ATBM does not lock partners in.



---



\## Language Discipline



When referencing ATBM, institutions should use:

\- descriptive language

\- non-promotional framing

\- accuracy over advocacy



ATBM is not a brand campaign.



---



\## Closing Position



ATBM exists to improve trust, not control people.



Institutions are welcome to learn.

They are not entitled to own.



---



End of institutional interface.
