\# What ATBM Is



ATBM stands for Aletheseal Trust Baseline Model.



It exists to answer one simple question:



How can people, institutions, and future generations trust that

systems were built honestly, documented properly, and can be examined

long after their creators are gone?



---



\## Why ATBM Exists



Many systems today:

\- cannot be inspected

\- cannot be explained

\- cannot be trusted over time

\- rely on authority instead of evidence



ATBM was created to change that.



It provides a way to build things so that:

\- evidence is preserved

\- decisions can be traced

\- mistakes remain visible

\- truth does not depend on reputation



---



\## What ATBM Is Not



ATBM is not:

\- a company

\- a product

\- a political movement

\- a surveillance system

\- a certification scheme

\- a way to control people



ATBM does not judge individuals.

It documents systems.



---



\## What ATBM Actually Does



ATBM defines how to:

\- record what was done

\- prove how it was done

\- show who decided what

\- preserve this information over time



It treats evidence as something that must survive:

\- leadership changes

\- institutional collapse

\- legal disputes

\- public scrutiny

\- historical distance



---



\## Who ATBM Is For



ATBM is for:

\- learners who want to understand systems

\- people rebuilding their lives and skills

\- educators teaching critical thinking

\- institutions seeking transparency

\- communities that value truth over authority



It is especially relevant where trust has been broken.



---



\## Education Without Gatekeeping



ATBM can be taught without:

\- formal qualifications

\- government accreditation

\- proprietary platforms



Learning ATBM means learning:

\- how systems work

\- how evidence is created

\- how claims are tested

\- how integrity is maintained



It teaches thinking, not compliance.



---



\## Accountability Without Punishment



ATBM does not punish failure.



It records:

\- what was attempted

\- what worked

\- what did not

\- why decisions were made



This allows learning instead of denial.



---



\## Long-Term Thinking



ATBM is designed for decades, not quarters.



It assumes:

\- people will change

\- organisations will fail

\- technology will evolve

\- records will be questioned



The model survives these changes.



---



\## Independence Matters



ATBM is intentionally independent.



No single organisation owns it.

No authority controls it.

No sponsor defines truth within it.



This independence is what gives it value.



---



\## A Simple Way to Understand ATBM



If someone asks what ATBM is, a correct answer is:



“ATBM is a way of building systems so that the truth about how they were made can still be known in the future.”



Nothing more is required.
