\# Language and Translation Layer



ATBM separates structure from language.



---



\## Canon Language



The canonical documents are written in:

\- precise English

\- non-idiomatic phrasing

\- minimal cultural reference



---



\## Translation Strategy



\- translate meaning, not wording

\- preserve structure

\- avoid colloquialisms



---



\## Localisation Rules



Local institutions may:

\- rename phases

\- adapt examples

\- contextualise narratives



Structural integrity must remain intact.



---



\## Ownership



Translations belong to the adopting institution.
