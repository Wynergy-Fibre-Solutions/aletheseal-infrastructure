\# Jurisdictional Adaptability



ATBM is jurisdiction-agnostic by design.



---



\## Legal Independence



ATBM:

\- does not issue legal judgements

\- does not store regulated data by default

\- does not assert authority



---



\## Deployment Models



\- offline-first

\- on-premise

\- sovereign cloud

\- hybrid



All are supported.



---



\## Compliance Strategy



ATBM aligns to:

\- local data protection law

\- local safeguarding requirements

\- local institutional oversight



ATBM does not override them.



---



\## Result



Adoption does not trigger legal reclassification.
