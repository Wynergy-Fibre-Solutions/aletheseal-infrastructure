\# Ethical Universality



ATBM is grounded in universally recognised principles.



---



\## Core Ethics



\- dignity

\- autonomy

\- non-coercion

\- reversibility

\- transparency



---



\## What Is Avoided



\- moral judgement

\- behavioural scoring

\- ideological framing



---



\## Cultural Respect



ATBM does not:

\- challenge belief systems

\- redefine morality

\- impose values



---



\## Safeguard



If a deployment violates these principles, it is non-compliant with ATBM.
