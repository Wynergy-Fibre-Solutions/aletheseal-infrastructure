\# Cross-Border Continuity



ATBM evidence travels with the individual.



---



\## Portability



\- learner-controlled artefacts

\- no central authority required

\- format remains consistent



---



\## Migration Scenarios



\- country to country

\- custody to community

\- education to employment



Continuity is preserved.



---



\## Verification



Evidence may be:

\- re-verified

\- re-contextualised

\- independently reviewed



No re-enrolment is required.



---



\## Impact



Mobility does not erase progress.
