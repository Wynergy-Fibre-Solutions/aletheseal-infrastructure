\# Cultural Neutrality Principles



ATBM must function without imposing worldview.



---



\## What ATBM Is Not



\- not ideological

\- not political

\- not religious

\- not nationalistic



---



\## Design Rule



ATBM structures process, not belief.



Beliefs, values, and culture remain local.



---



\## Language Neutrality



\- no moral hierarchy

\- no cultural superiority

\- no behavioural norm enforcement



---



\## Outcome



Learners from any background can engage without identity conflict.
