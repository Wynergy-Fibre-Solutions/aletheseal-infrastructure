\# ATBM Institution Mapping Note  

\## Coexistence Without Replacement



This document explains how ATBM integrates with existing institutions without replacing, overriding, or competing with them.



---



\## Core Position



ATBM is not an institutional replacement.



It is a \*\*supportive framework\*\* that operates alongside existing systems to reduce harm, clarify consent, and strengthen agency.



---



\## What ATBM Does Not Do



ATBM does not:

\- replace statutory authority

\- override policy

\- alter legal obligations

\- redefine institutional roles

\- assume governance control



Existing structures remain intact.



---



\## Where ATBM Sits



ATBM operates in the spaces between:

\- policy and practice

\- authority and consent

\- instruction and autonomy

\- safeguarding and agency



It adds clarity without removing control from lawful authorities.



---



\## Compatibility with Existing Systems



ATBM is compatible with:

\- education frameworks

\- safeguarding standards

\- custodial environments

\- probation services

\- community programmes

\- academic institutions



Because it does not demand exclusivity.



---



\## Authority Alignment



Institutional authority:

\- remains unchanged

\- retains decision-making power

\- enforces lawful boundaries



ATBM:

\- clarifies consent

\- defines exit rights

\- prevents informal coercion



These functions are complementary, not competitive.



---



\## Risk and Compliance Perspective



ATBM reduces institutional risk by:

\- documenting consent clarity

\- separating participation from outcomes

\- preventing power misuse

\- enabling independent inspection



It does not introduce unmanaged liability.



---



\## Safeguarding Integration



ATBM aligns with safeguarding by:

\- prioritising informed consent

\- protecting refusal

\- enabling safe exit

\- discouraging dependency



It avoids surveillance-based safeguarding models.



---



\## Operational Use Cases



Institutions may use ATBM as:

\- a teaching framework

\- a discussion scaffold

\- a reflection tool

\- a boundary clarification aid



Use is modular and optional.



---



\## Inspection and Review



ATBM is designed to be:

\- openly inspected

\- externally reviewed

\- challenged without retaliation



It welcomes scrutiny.



---



\## Staff Role Protection



ATBM does not:

\- undermine staff authority

\- expose staff to undue risk

\- create parallel power structures



It protects staff by clarifying boundaries.



---



\## Exit and Non-Participation



Institutional participation in ATBM:

\- is optional

\- can be paused

\- can be discontinued



Adoption does not create dependency.



---



\## Final Principle



ATBM strengthens institutions by reducing hidden harm.



It succeeds by \*\*not\*\* trying to replace what already exists.



---



End of institution mapping note.
