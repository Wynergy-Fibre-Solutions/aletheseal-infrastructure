\# Intergenerational Continuity Protocol (P.6)



\## Purpose

This protocol ensures ATBM survives generational change without dilution,

authoritarian drift, or loss of meaning.



ATBM must remain intelligible to people who never met its originators.



---



\## 1. Succession Without Inheritance



ATBM is not inherited.

It is re-learned by each generation.



No individual, family, organisation, or lineage

may claim succession rights.



Continuity is earned through understanding, not appointment.



---



\## 2. Teachability as the Continuity Test



If ATBM cannot be taught clearly, it has failed.



Each generation must be able to:

\- Learn ATBM without prior allegiance

\- Question ATBM without penalty

\- Leave ATBM without consequence

\- Teach ATBM without authority



Teachability overrides tradition.



---



\## 3. No Canon Expansion Without Time



New material may be created.

Canon may not be expanded rapidly.



Rules:

\- Canon additions require long-term testing

\- Minimum multi-year observation periods

\- Independent review across contexts

\- Explicit non-replacement of original canon



Stability precedes growth.



---



\## 4. Language Drift Control



ATBM language must remain:

\- Plain

\- Non-mystical

\- Non-technical where unnecessary

\- Culturally translatable



If language becomes elite, obscure, or coded,

continuity is considered broken.



---



\## 5. Memory Without Dogma



ATBM retains memory through:

\- Documents

\- Public archives

\- Teaching artefacts

\- Open repositories



ATBM does not retain memory through:

\- Ritual

\- Rank

\- Titles

\- Personality reverence



Memory serves clarity, not belief.



---



\## 6. Generational Reset Right



Each generation holds the right to:

\- Re-evaluate relevance

\- Reduce scope

\- Pause teaching

\- Walk away entirely



ATBM must justify itself repeatedly or dissolve.



---



\## 7. Failure Is an Acceptable Outcome



If ATBM:

\- Becomes coercive

\- Loses ethical grounding

\- Cannot be safely taught

\- Is misused at scale



Then discontinuation is valid.



Persistence is not the goal.

Integrity is.



---



\## 8. Continuity Guardians



Continuity guardians:

\- Monitor drift

\- Flag harm

\- Trigger review

\- Do not govern



Guardians may dissolve themselves if unnecessary.
