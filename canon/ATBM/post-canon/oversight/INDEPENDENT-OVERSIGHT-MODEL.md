\# Independent Oversight Model (P.3)



\## Purpose

This document defines how ATBM remains safe, ethical, and lawful once deployed

beyond its originators. Oversight is external, independent, and permanent.



No individual, organisation, or founder retains control.



---



\## 1. Principle of External Authority



ATBM may never self-police.



Oversight must be:

\- Independent of creators and facilitators

\- Able to suspend or revoke use

\- Accessible to learners directly

\- Protected from retaliation or influence



Internal review is insufficient.



---



\## 2. Oversight Bodies



Acceptable oversight bodies include:

\- Independent charities or CICs

\- Academic ethics boards

\- Prison education review panels

\- Safeguarding organisations

\- Human rights or ombuds services



Multiple bodies may coexist.



No single body may monopolise oversight.



---



\## 3. Oversight Powers



Oversight bodies are empowered to:

\- Suspend facilitation immediately

\- Audit teaching materials and sessions

\- Receive confidential learner reports

\- Mandate corrective action

\- Withdraw ATBM alignment status

\- Publish findings without permission



Creators may not veto outcomes.



---



\## 4. Trigger Conditions for Review



Automatic review is triggered if:

\- A learner files a safeguarding concern

\- A facilitator breaches safety addenda

\- Participation becomes compulsory

\- ATBM is tied to punishment or reward

\- Institutional pressure overrides autonomy

\- Any coercion is alleged



No burden of proof lies on the learner.



---



\## 5. Transparency Requirements



All deployments must disclose:

\- Oversight body identities

\- Reporting pathways

\- Suspension criteria

\- Learner rights summaries

\- Exit guarantees



This information must be provided before participation.



---



\## 6. Protection Against Institutional Capture



ATBM may not be:

\- Rebranded as institutional doctrine

\- Used to enforce compliance

\- Integrated into disciplinary systems

\- Framed as rehabilitation requirement

\- Used as evidence of reform or risk



Educational use only. Always voluntary.



---



\## 7. Revocation and Sunset



Oversight bodies may:

\- Revoke ATBM use permanently

\- Require sunset clauses

\- Mandate external replacement



ATBM does not demand perpetuity.



---



\## 8. Canon Survival Clause



If independent oversight is refused, obstructed, or removed:

\- ATBM use becomes invalid

\- Canon alignment is withdrawn

\- Continued use is unauthorised



The canon exists only with oversight.
