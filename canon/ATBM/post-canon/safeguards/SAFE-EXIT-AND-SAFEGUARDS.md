\# ATBM Safe Exit and Safeguards



This page explains your rights if you encounter ATBM in any setting.



You do not have to participate.

You can stop at any time.



---



\## Your Right to Say No



You may refuse:

\- participation

\- discussion

\- activities

\- continuation



You do not need to give a reason.



---



\## Your Right to Leave



You may:

\- leave a session

\- pause involvement

\- disengage permanently



Leaving is allowed.

Leaving is safe.



---



\## No Penalties



Refusing or leaving:

\- does not affect your status

\- does not remove opportunities

\- does not lead to punishment

\- does not count against you



If this is not true in practice, the system is being misused.



---



\## No Hidden Conditions



Participation is never required for:

\- approval

\- progression

\- privileges

\- access to services



Any suggestion otherwise is incorrect.



---



\## Facilitator Limits



Facilitators may not:

\- pressure you to stay

\- question your refusal

\- imply consequences

\- record refusal as failure



If this happens, it is a breach of ATBM.



---



\## If You Feel Unsafe



If you feel:

\- pressured

\- confused about your rights

\- afraid to refuse or leave



You can:

\- stop immediately

\- leave the session

\- ask for this page to be reviewed



You do not need to explain.



---



\## Safeguarding Without Control



ATBM protects people by:

\- making exit clear

\- preventing coercion

\- limiting authority

\- encouraging transparency



It does not use surveillance or punishment.



---



\## Misuse Warning



ATBM is invalid if:

\- refusal leads to consequences

\- exit is discouraged

\- participation is implied as mandatory



These are serious breaches.



---



\## Final Reminder



You owe this system nothing.



If it helps, use it.

If it does not, walk away.



That choice is always yours.



---



End of safe exit and safeguards sheet.
