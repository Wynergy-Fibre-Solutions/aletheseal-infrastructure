\# Facilitator Safety Addendum (P.2.1)



\## Purpose

This document defines non-negotiable safety boundaries for anyone facilitating

learning under the ATBM canon. These rules exist to prevent coercion, harm,

authority abuse, and institutional misuse.



This addendum overrides all other teaching guidance.



---



\## 1. Absolute Red Lines (Never Permitted)



A facilitator MUST NOT:



\- Apply pressure to continue participation.

\- Imply moral, intellectual, or personal superiority.

\- Frame disagreement as ignorance, resistance, or pathology.

\- Use deprivation, reward, status, or access as leverage.

\- Present the canon as “truth”, “enlightenment”, or “inevitable”.

\- Discourage external relationships, education, or oversight.

\- Require disclosure of personal trauma, beliefs, or history.

\- Record, monitor, or profile learners beyond consented learning artefacts.

\- Act as gatekeeper to freedom, progression, or validation.



Violation of any red line requires immediate suspension.



---



\## 2. Mandatory Stop Conditions



Teaching MUST STOP immediately if:



\- A learner expresses discomfort, confusion, or pressure.

\- A learner requests to pause, exit, or disengage.

\- Emotional distress is observed or disclosed.

\- Group dynamics shift toward conformity or silence.

\- The facilitator is perceived as an authority figure.

\- Institutional objectives conflict with learner autonomy.



No explanation is required from the learner.



---



\## 3. Non-Participation and Exit Rights



Every learner has the unconditional right to:



\- Decline participation without justification.

\- Leave sessions permanently or temporarily.

\- Re-enter later without penalty or status loss.

\- Observe without engaging.

\- Disagree publicly or privately.



Exit must never be recorded as failure, resistance, or risk.



---



\## 4. Facilitator Role Definition



A facilitator is:



\- A temporary guide, not a leader.

\- Replaceable and non-essential.

\- Accountable to learners, not above them.

\- Bound by restraint, not influence.



Facilitators do not interpret meaning on behalf of learners.



---



\## 5. Safeguards Against Authority Drift



To prevent authority accumulation:



\- No facilitator may teach the same cohort indefinitely.

\- No facilitator may certify, rank, or assess learners.

\- No exclusive language, symbols, or identity markers permitted.

\- No isolation from external education or frameworks.

\- Independent oversight must always be accessible.



---



\## 6. Reporting and Escalation



Learners must be informed, in writing, of:



\- How to report facilitator misconduct.

\- Independent bodies able to receive concerns.

\- Guaranteed protection from retaliation.



Reports suspend facilitation pending review.



---



\## 7. Canon Integrity Clause



If this addendum is violated, ignored, or bypassed:



\- The session loses ATBM alignment.

\- Certification (if any) is void.

\- Continued use of the canon is unauthorised.



The canon does not survive coercion.
