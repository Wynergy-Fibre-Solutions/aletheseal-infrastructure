\# Global Portability Framework (P.4)



\## Purpose

This framework guarantees ATBM can operate ethically and lawfully across

countries, institutions, and cultures without imposing ideology, governance,

or technical dependency.



ATBM must adapt to jurisdictions, not override them.



---



\## 1. Jurisdictional Neutrality



ATBM:

\- Makes no claims of legal supremacy

\- Does not conflict with domestic law

\- Defers to local safeguarding rules

\- Allows local compliance overlays



If a jurisdiction prohibits an element, that element is withdrawn locally.



---



\## 2. Cultural Non-Imposition



ATBM:

\- Avoids political, religious, or ideological framing

\- Uses neutral language by default

\- Permits contextual translation without reinterpretation

\- Prohibits value enforcement



No participant is required to adopt beliefs, identities, or narratives.



---



\## 3. Modular Deployment



ATBM components are:

\- Independently deployable

\- Independently removable

\- Independently auditable



No “all-or-nothing” requirement exists.



Institutions may deploy only what is lawful and safe locally.



---



\## 4. Exit Without Penalty



All jurisdictions must guarantee:

\- Immediate exit on request

\- No impact on legal status, benefits, or conditions

\- No reporting of non-participation

\- No adverse inference



Portability includes the right to stop.



---



\## 5. Translation and Interpretation Controls



Translations must:

\- Preserve rights language verbatim

\- Avoid interpretive expansion

\- Be reviewed by independent bodies

\- Declare limitations openly



No localisation may weaken protections.



---



\## 6. Cross-Border Evidence Integrity



Where evidence or artefacts exist:

\- Ownership remains with the learner

\- Storage complies with local data law

\- Export requires explicit consent

\- Deletion rights are absolute



No global evidence aggregation is permitted.



---



\## 7. Suspension Across Borders



If ATBM is suspended in one jurisdiction:

\- That suspension is visible globally

\- Reasons are published

\- Other jurisdictions are alerted

\- No concealment is allowed



Transparency prevents harm replication.



---



\## 8. Non-Export Clause



ATBM may not be exported:

\- As a policy instrument

\- As a control mechanism

\- As a compliance framework

\- As a surveillance tool



Education only. Always voluntary.
