\# ATBM Facilitator Guide  

\## Teaching Without Authority Abuse



This guide defines how ATBM may be facilitated without coercion, domination, dependency creation, or informal punishment.



Facilitation is a responsibility, not a position of power.



---



\## Core Duty of a Facilitator



Your role is to:

\- hold space

\- provide clarity

\- protect choice

\- prevent harm



You are not here to:

\- lead belief

\- extract compliance

\- produce outcomes

\- evaluate worth



---



\## Consent Is Ongoing



Participation must remain:

\- voluntary

\- informed

\- revocable at any time



Silence is not consent.

Attendance is not consent.



Check regularly without pressure.



---



\## Authority Boundaries



You may:

\- explain concepts

\- invite discussion

\- clarify misunderstandings

\- pause unsafe dynamics



You may not:

\- pressure participation

\- reward compliance

\- penalise refusal

\- imply consequences



---



\## Neutral Language Requirement



Use language that:

\- invites

\- explains

\- clarifies



Avoid language that:

\- frames resistance as a problem

\- implies moral superiority

\- suggests progress depends on agreement



---



\## Handling Disagreement



Disagreement is:

\- expected

\- permitted

\- valuable



Your role is to:

\- allow it

\- keep it respectful

\- prevent escalation



Never correct belief.  

Clarify structure only.



---



\## Managing Silence



Silence may mean:

\- reflection

\- uncertainty

\- disengagement

\- refusal



All are acceptable.



Do not force contribution.



---



\## Power Awareness



Always assume you hold more power than you think.



Power may come from:

\- role

\- context

\- environment

\- institutional backing



Act accordingly.



---



\## Vulnerable Environments



In prisons, probation, care, or education:

\- reiterate exit rights frequently

\- separate participation from outcomes

\- document consent clarity



Never assume safety because harm is invisible.



---



\## Emotional Boundaries



You are not a therapist.



If emotional distress arises:

\- pause the activity

\- acknowledge feelings

\- refer appropriately if needed



Do not attempt treatment.



---



\## Safeguarding Without Surveillance



Safeguarding is achieved through:

\- transparency

\- clear exit paths

\- consent checks



Not through monitoring or reporting participants.



---



\## Ending a Session



Always end by:

\- restating exit rights

\- clarifying no obligation to return

\- thanking participation without praise or judgement



---



\## Self-Check Questions



Before, during, and after facilitation, ask:

\- Am I trying to persuade?

\- Am I uncomfortable with dissent?

\- Am I equating silence with failure?

\- Am I creating dependency?



If yes, stop and recalibrate.



---



\## Final Principle



If facilitation requires pressure, it is not facilitation.



ATBM only works when people remain free.



---



End of facilitator guide.
