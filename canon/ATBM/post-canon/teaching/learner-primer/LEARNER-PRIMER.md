\# ATBM Learner Primer



This document explains what ATBM is, what it is not, and what your rights are if you choose to engage with it.



You are not required to participate.

You are free to stop at any time.



---



\## What This Is



ATBM is a way of thinking and working that helps people:

\- understand systems

\- make informed choices

\- act with integrity

\- protect their own agency



It is about clarity, not control.



---



\## What This Is Not



ATBM is not:

\- a belief system

\- a test of character

\- a therapy programme

\- a punishment or reward scheme

\- a requirement for approval



You do not need to agree with it.

You do not need to like it.

You do not need to finish it.



---



\## Your Rights



If you engage with ATBM, you have the right to:

\- ask questions

\- disagree

\- pause

\- leave

\- refuse any activity



You never have to explain why.



---



\## Participation Is Optional



You can:

\- take part fully

\- take part partially

\- observe only

\- stop completely



All of these choices are valid.



---



\## No Hidden Consequences



Choosing not to participate:

\- does not affect your status

\- does not remove opportunities

\- does not count against you



There are no penalties for refusal.



---



\## How Learning Works Here



Learning is:

\- discussion-based

\- reflective

\- practical where relevant



There are no trick questions.

There is no right personality.

There is no pressure to perform.



---



\## Discomfort vs Harm



You may encounter:

\- challenging ideas

\- unfamiliar perspectives

\- moments of discomfort



This is not harm.



Harm would be:

\- pressure to comply

\- fear of consequences

\- loss of dignity

\- inability to leave



If anything feels unsafe, you can stop.



---



\## Authority Limits



Facilitators may:

\- guide discussion

\- explain concepts

\- maintain basic boundaries



They may not:

\- force participation

\- judge your worth

\- pressure agreement

\- punish refusal



---



\## If You Feel Uncomfortable



You can:

\- speak up

\- ask for clarification

\- pause participation

\- leave the session



No explanation is required.



---



\## What ATBM Is Trying to Do



ATBM exists to:

\- reduce harm

\- increase understanding

\- support autonomy

\- allow people to think clearly in complex systems



Nothing more.



---



\## Final Reminder



You owe this system nothing.



If it helps, use it.

If it does not, walk away.



That choice is always yours.



---



End of learner primer.
