\# Independent Custodianship Protocol (P.5)



\## Purpose

This protocol prevents ATBM from being captured, centralised, or repurposed

by power, money, authority, or ideology.



ATBM must outlive its creators without becoming owned.



---



\## 1. Custodianship Model



ATBM operates under a custodianship model, not ownership.



Custodians:

\- Preserve integrity

\- Prevent misuse

\- Cannot alter core principles

\- Cannot extract exclusive benefit



Custodians are stewards, not controllers.



---



\## 2. No Single Point of Control



ATBM may not be:

\- Owned by one entity

\- Governed by one board

\- Licensed exclusively

\- Branded as proprietary doctrine



Any attempt at consolidation invalidates custodianship.



---



\## 3. Multi-Jurisdictional Custodian Pool



Custodians must:

\- Exist in multiple countries

\- Operate independently

\- Publish public attestations

\- Rotate participation periodically



No jurisdiction may dominate.



---



\## 4. Founder Displacement Safeguard



Founders:

\- Hold no permanent authority

\- May be overridden by custodial consensus

\- Cannot revoke learner rights

\- Cannot monetise participation status



ATBM must survive founder absence.



---



\## 5. Anti-Commercial Capture



ATBM may not be:

\- Bundled with coercive services

\- Tied to funding conditions

\- Used as a gatekeeping credential

\- Marketed as compliance technology



Education may be funded. Participation may not be sold.



---



\## 6. Fork Without Hostility



If disagreement occurs:

\- Peaceful forks are permitted

\- Canon integrity must be preserved

\- Forks must declare divergence

\- No suppression is allowed



Pluralism is protection.



---



\## 7. Transparency of Custodians



Each custodian must publish:

\- Identity and jurisdiction

\- Conflict disclosures

\- Scope of involvement

\- Exit statements on departure



Opacity is disallowed.



---



\## 8. Custodial Failure Protocol



If custodians fail:

\- Emergency suspension applies

\- Public notice is issued

\- Independent review is triggered

\- Learner protections remain active



ATBM pauses rather than corrupts.



---



\## 9. Immutable Learner Rights



No custodian may:

\- Remove exit rights

\- Add surveillance

\- Impose obligation

\- Retain data without consent



Learner rights override all governance.
