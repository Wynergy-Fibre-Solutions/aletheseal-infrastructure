\# Long-Term Archival Policy



ATBM must be preserved for future generations.



---



\## Archival Requirements



\- multiple physical locations

\- multiple custodians

\- periodic format review



---



\## Recommended Intervals



\- integrity check every 12 months

\- format review every 5 years

\- full audit every 10 years



---



\## Archive Neutrality



Archives must not:

\- alter content

\- add interpretation

\- remove context



---



\## Purpose



Preservation, not control.
