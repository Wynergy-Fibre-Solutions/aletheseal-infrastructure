\# Institutional Memory Framework



This document defines how ATBM preserves intent, structure, and integrity across time.



---



\## 1. Memory Beyond Individuals



ATBM must never depend on:

\- founders

\- key staff

\- charismatic leadership

\- undocumented knowledge



All critical knowledge must be written.



---



\## 2. Canon over Commentary



The ATBM Canon is the source of truth.



\- meetings do not override canon

\- interpretations do not replace structure

\- customisations must be documented



---



\## 3. Change Recording



All changes must include:

\- rationale

\- scope

\- impact

\- reversibility assessment



Silent drift is prohibited.



---



\## 4. Succession Safety



A new custodian must be able to:

\- understand ATBM without oral briefing

\- verify integrity independently

\- continue operation lawfully



---



\## 5. Time-Resilient Language



Canonical documents must:

\- avoid contemporary slang

\- avoid policy-specific references

\- avoid technology hype



Clarity must survive decades.



---



\## 6. Preservation Formats



ATBM artefacts must exist as:

\- plain text

\- open formats

\- offline-readable copies



No single platform dependency is permitted.



---



\## 7. Decentralised Custody



Copies of the canon may be held by:

\- institutions

\- libraries

\- archives

\- oversight bodies



No exclusive custody exists.



---



\## 8. Failure Scenarios



ATBM must remain usable if:

\- funding ends

\- staff leave

\- servers are decommissioned

\- vendors disappear



The framework must outlive infrastructure.



---



\## 9. Revival Capability



A paused deployment must be restartable without:

\- re-licensing

\- re-authorisation

\- re-certification



Continuity is intrinsic.



---



\## 10. Integrity Checks



Periodic reviews must verify:

\- canonical completeness

\- evidence consistency

\- ethical alignment



Drift must be corrected, not normalised.



---



\## 11. Stewardship Role



Stewards protect structure, not control outcomes.



Authority is custodial, not directive.



---



\## 12. Final Safeguard



If ATBM becomes corrupted, fragmented, or misused,

this document authorises reversion to the last known

intact canonical state.
