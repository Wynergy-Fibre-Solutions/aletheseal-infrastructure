\# ATBM Handover Protocol



This protocol governs transfer of stewardship.



---



\## Purpose



To ensure ATBM survives leadership and organisational change.



---



\## Minimum Handover Package



\- full ATBM canon

\- evidence archive

\- integrity hashes

\- deployment notes

\- adaptation records



---



\## Prohibited Practices



\- undocumented changes

\- verbal-only transfer

\- selective disclosure

\- reinterpretation without record



---



\## Verification Step



Incoming stewards must:

\- validate canonical integrity

\- confirm evidence continuity

\- acknowledge ethical boundaries



---



\## Failure Clause



If handover is incomplete, stewardship is not valid.
