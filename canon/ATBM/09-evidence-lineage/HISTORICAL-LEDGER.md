\# ATBM Historical Ledger



This ledger indexes all canonical phases and evidence.



---



\## Ledger Structure



Each entry includes:

\- phase identifier

\- date

\- artefact references

\- evidence location

\- verification method



---



\## Ledger Rules



\- entries are append-only

\- renumbering is prohibited

\- gaps must be explained



---



\## Purpose



Enable complete reconstruction of ATBM at any point in time.
