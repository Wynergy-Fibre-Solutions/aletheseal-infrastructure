\# External Review Protocol



This protocol governs independent examination of ATBM.



---



\## Review Scope



Reviewers may assess:

\- canon integrity

\- evidence correspondence

\- ethical compliance

\- governance adherence



---



\## Access Model



\- read-only

\- non-destructive

\- time-bound if required



---



\## Reviewer Independence



Reviewers must:

\- declare conflicts

\- avoid modification

\- document findings



---



\## Outcome



Reviews strengthen ATBM legitimacy without altering history.
