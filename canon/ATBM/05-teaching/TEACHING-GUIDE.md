\# Teaching ATBM



ATBM can be taught without formal accreditation.



---



\## Teaching Contexts



\- prisons and probation

\- charities and CICs

\- universities and colleges

\- independent learning

\- online or offline



---



\## Teaching Principles



\- no assumed prior knowledge

\- evidence-first learning

\- reflection over testing

\- progress over speed



---



\## Core Teaching Units



1\. Understanding trust

2\. Building capability

3\. Creating evidence

4\. Ethical responsibility

5\. Long-term thinking



---



\## Assessment



Assessment is:

\- artefact-based

\- process-documented

\- peer-reviewable



No high-stakes testing is required.



---



\## Safeguards



Teaching must:

\- respect vulnerability

\- avoid coercion

\- remain voluntary

\- prioritise wellbeing



---



\## Educator Role



Educators are:

\- facilitators

\- reviewers

\- guides



Not gatekeepers.



---



\## Learner Agency



Learners own:

\- their evidence

\- their narrative

\- their pace



This is essential.



---



\## Closing Note



Teaching ATBM is teaching responsibility.
