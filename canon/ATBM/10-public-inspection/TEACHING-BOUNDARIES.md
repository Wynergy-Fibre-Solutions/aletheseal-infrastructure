\# Teaching Safety Boundaries



ATBM teaching must remain ethical.



---



\## Prohibited Practices



\- coercive instruction

\- behavioural enforcement

\- outcome guarantees

\- ideological framing



---



\## Safeguards



Teaching must:

\- respect autonomy

\- preserve dignity

\- allow withdrawal

\- remain transparent



---



\## Enforcement



Breaches invalidate teaching legitimacy.
