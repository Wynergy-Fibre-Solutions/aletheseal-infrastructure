\# ATBM Public Canon



This document defines how ATBM is presented for inspection and learning.



---



\## Purpose



To expose ATBM structure, intent, and safeguards

without requiring private explanation.



---



\## Canon Scope



The public canon includes:

\- structural principles

\- governance layers

\- ethical boundaries

\- evidence methods

\- continuity guarantees



It excludes sensitive deployment data.



---



\## Inspection Model



Anyone may:

\- read the canon

\- trace phases

\- inspect evidence references

\- question assumptions



No authority approval required.



---



\## Non-Marketing Rule



The canon:

\- does not persuade

\- does not promise outcomes

\- does not frame benefit claims



It presents structure only.



---



\## Critique Readiness



Criticism is expected.



ATBM:

\- tolerates disagreement

\- preserves dissent

\- documents counterpoints



Strength is demonstrated through exposure.
