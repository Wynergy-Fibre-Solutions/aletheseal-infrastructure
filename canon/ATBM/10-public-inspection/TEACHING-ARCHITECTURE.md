\# Teaching Canon Architecture



This document defines how ATBM is taught without authority dependence.



---



\## Teaching Principles



\- structure before application

\- evidence before interpretation

\- autonomy before instruction



---



\## Modular Teaching Units



Each phase may be taught independently:

\- governance

\- trust

\- evidence

\- continuity

\- ethics



No linear dependency is required.



---



\## Instructor Role



Instructors:

\- explain structure

\- facilitate inspection

\- do not impose interpretation



---



\## Learner Role



Learners:

\- interrogate assumptions

\- verify evidence

\- draw conclusions independently
