\# Public Challenge Protocol



This protocol governs challenge without destabilisation.



---



\## Right to Challenge



Any party may:

\- question assumptions

\- identify weaknesses

\- propose alternatives



---



\## Recording Challenges



Challenges must:

\- be documented

\- remain visible

\- not be suppressed



---



\## Response Model



Responses:

\- address substance

\- cite evidence

\- avoid defensiveness



---



\## Integrity Rule



Challenges strengthen ATBM if preserved.
