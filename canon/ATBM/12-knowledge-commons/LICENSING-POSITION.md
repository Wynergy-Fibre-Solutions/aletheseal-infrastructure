\# Licensing Position



ATBM adopts a commons-aligned posture.



---



\## Allowed



\- study

\- teaching

\- non-distortive reuse

\- critical analysis



---



\## Prohibited



\- misrepresentation

\- claim of exclusive authorship

\- removal of ethical safeguards

\- erasure of lineage



---



\## Attribution



Attribution preserves history,

not control.



---



\## Principle



Freedom to learn must not enable distortion.
