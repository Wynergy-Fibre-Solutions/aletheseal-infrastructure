\# Archival Replication Protocol



This protocol governs how ATBM is mirrored and preserved.



---



\## Replication Targets



ATBM should be mirrored to:

\- independent repositories

\- academic archives

\- libraries

\- offline media



---



\## Format Requirements



\- plain text

\- open standards

\- non-proprietary formats



---



\## Integrity Safeguards



Each archive copy must include:

\- canonical index

\- evidence lineage

\- integrity hashes



---



\## Failure Tolerance



If one archive fails,

others must remain usable.
