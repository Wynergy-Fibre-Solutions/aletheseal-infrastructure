\# ATBM Knowledge Commons



This document defines ATBM as a civilisational knowledge artefact.



---



\## Purpose



To preserve ATBM beyond institutional, political, or economic control.



---



\## Commons Definition



ATBM exists as:

\- a documented framework

\- a teachable structure

\- a verifiable historical record



It is not proprietary knowledge.



---



\## Access Principle



Anyone may:

\- read the canon

\- study the structure

\- analyse the evolution

\- critique the framework



No permission is required.



---



\## Non-Exclusivity



No organisation may claim:

\- exclusive authority

\- final interpretation

\- permanent control



Custody is temporary.



---



\## Preservation Priority



Preservation outranks:

\- branding

\- monetisation

\- expansion



Loss of integrity is unacceptable.
