\# Civilisational Failure Contingency



This document defines ATBM survival under extreme conditions.



---



\## Assumptions



\- institutions may collapse

\- networks may fragment

\- digital platforms may fail



---



\## Survivability Design



ATBM must:

\- exist offline

\- be readable without tools

\- require no authentication



---



\## Re-discovery Model



Future readers must be able to:

\- understand intent

\- reconstruct structure

\- verify authenticity



Without contacting originators.



---



\## Final Safeguard



If ATBM survives as text alone,

this phase has succeeded.
