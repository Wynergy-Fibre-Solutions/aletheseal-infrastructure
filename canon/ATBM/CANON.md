\# ATBM Canon



The ATBM Canon defines the authoritative body of knowledge for the Adaptive Trust Baseline Model.



This canon exists to:

\- teach

\- critique

\- replicate

\- preserve



It is not marketing. It is reference.



---



\## Canon Structure



\### 01 — Foundations

Why ATBM exists, what problems it addresses, and its conceptual roots.



\### 02 — Capability

How human capability is defined, developed, and evidenced.



\### 03 — Evidence

How proof is created, sealed, verified, and preserved.



\### 04 — Ethics

The ethical boundaries that govern use, deployment, and teaching.



\### 05 — Teaching

How ATBM is taught in custodial, academic, and independent settings.



---



\## Canon Principles



\- Evidence over assertion

\- Process over credential

\- Ethics over expediency

\- Learning over compliance

\- Trust through verification
