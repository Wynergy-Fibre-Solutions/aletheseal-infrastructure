\# ATBM Foundations



ATBM was created in response to systemic failures in how trust, education, and rehabilitation are measured.



---



\## The Problem



Modern systems:

\- confuse credentials with capability

\- reward compliance over understanding

\- discard evidence of growth

\- fail people during transition points



Especially:

\- prison release

\- career change

\- social reintegration



---



\## The Insight



Trust is not a credential.

Trust is demonstrated behaviour, evidenced over time.



---



\## Core Premise



If learning, effort, and capability are:

\- documented

\- evidenced

\- verifiable



Then trust can be rebuilt without gatekeepers.



---



\## What ATBM Is



\- a trust baseline

\- a capability framework

\- an evidence system

\- an educational methodology



---



\## What ATBM Is Not



\- a qualification

\- a replacement for education

\- a shortcut

\- a compliance tool



---



\## Foundational Values



\- dignity

\- accountability

\- transparency

\- patience

\- proof



---



\## Historical Context



ATBM draws from:

\- education theory

\- rehabilitation practice

\- digital evidence systems

\- ethical governance



It rejects extractive models.



---



\## Outcome



ATBM creates continuity where systems create fragmentation.
