\# Generational Handoff Charter



This charter governs ATBM transfer between generations.



---



\## Purpose



To ensure continuity without control concentration.



---



\## Handoff Requirements



A valid handoff includes:

\- full canon

\- evidence lineage

\- ethical commitments

\- stewardship acknowledgements



---



\## Prohibited Actions



\- selective inheritance

\- narrative rewriting

\- authority hoarding

\- undocumented reinterpretation



---



\## Acceptance Condition



Incoming stewards must explicitly accept:

\- ethical obligations

\- reviewability

\- non-ownership status



---



\## Failure Clause



If conditions are not met, the handoff is invalid.
