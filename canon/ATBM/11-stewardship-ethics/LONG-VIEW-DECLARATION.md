\# Long-View Declaration



ATBM is designed for timelines longer than any organisation.



---



\## Time Horizon



ATBM assumes:

\- decades, not quarters

\- generations, not funding cycles

\- stewardship, not control



---



\## Responsibility



Each generation holds ATBM in trust

for the next.



---



\## Measurement of Success



Success is measured by:

\- continuity

\- integrity

\- ethical resilience



Not by scale or dominance.



---



\## Final Statement



If ATBM remains useful, honest, and intact

after those who built it are gone,

this declaration has succeeded.
