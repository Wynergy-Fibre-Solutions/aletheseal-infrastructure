\# Dissent Handling Protocol



This protocol governs disagreement between verifiers.



---



\## Right to Disagree



Multiple verifiers may:

\- reach different conclusions

\- highlight different risks

\- interpret impacts differently



---



\## Preservation Rule



Dissenting reports must:

\- remain accessible

\- not be suppressed

\- not be merged into consensus



---



\## Integrity Principle



Disagreement strengthens trust if preserved transparently.
