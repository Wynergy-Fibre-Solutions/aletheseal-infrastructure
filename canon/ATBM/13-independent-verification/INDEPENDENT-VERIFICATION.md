\# Independent Verification Framework



This document defines how ATBM integrity is verified by independent bodies.



---



\## Purpose



To allow third parties to verify ATBM without reliance on creators or operators.



---



\## Independence Requirement



Verification bodies must:

\- have no operational control

\- have no commercial dependence

\- have no editorial authority



Independence is mandatory.



---



\## Scope of Verification



Verifiers may assess:

\- canonical completeness

\- evidence lineage integrity

\- ethical compliance

\- governance adherence



They do not dictate outcomes.



---



\## Evidence-First Model



Verification relies on:

\- public canon

\- evidence artefacts

\- integrity hashes

\- documented lineage



No privileged access is required.



---



\## Transparency Rule



Verification findings must be:

\- documented

\- attributable

\- publicly accessible where lawful



Silence is not validation.



---



\## Non-Endorsement Principle



Verification does not equal endorsement.



Verification confirms integrity, not approval.
