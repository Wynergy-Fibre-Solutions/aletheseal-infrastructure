\# Verification Process Protocol



This protocol defines how verification is conducted.



---



\## Step 1 — Canon Review



\- confirm completeness

\- confirm structural coherence

\- confirm version lineage



---



\## Step 2 — Evidence Validation



\- verify artefact hashes

\- confirm storage references

\- test reproducibility where applicable



---



\## Step 3 — Ethical Review



\- confirm safeguard presence

\- assess risk boundaries

\- identify ethical drift



---



\## Step 4 — Findings Publication



\- publish findings

\- cite evidence

\- state limitations



---



\## Rule



Verification must be reproducible by another verifier.
