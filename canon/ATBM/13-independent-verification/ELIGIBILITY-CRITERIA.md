\# Verification Eligibility Criteria



This document defines who may act as an ATBM verifier.



---



\## Eligible Bodies



\- universities

\- research institutes

\- libraries and archives

\- civil society organisations

\- inspection bodies



---



\## Ineligible Bodies



\- direct operators of ATBM

\- commercial licensors

\- political actors

\- entities with financial leverage



---



\## Conflict Disclosure



All verifiers must disclose:

\- funding sources

\- affiliations

\- prior involvement



Undeclared conflicts invalidate verification.
